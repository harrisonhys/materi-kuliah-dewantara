# Pertemuan 16: UAS Integrated IT Project Management Plan dan Defense

> **Evaluasi:** UAS • **Bobot RPS:** 30% • **Luaran:** Integrated PMP dan defense

## 1. 🎯 Tujuan Evaluasi

UAS mengukur kemampuan mengintegrasikan initiation, planning, risk/quality/resource/communication, delivery approach, monitoring/control, change, dan closure menjadi sistem pengelolaan proyek TI yang konsisten, realistis, evidence-based, dan profesional.

## 2. 📖 Skenario

Tim melakukan governance defense di hadapan sponsor/steering committee simulatif. Panel memutuskan kesiapan proyek dan menantang asumsi, dependency, trade-off, risk, serta control.

## 3. Artefak Wajib

- Project Charter dan feasibility summary;
- Stakeholder Register/engagement;
- Scope Statement, WBS, dictionary;
- Schedule/Gantt, network, critical path/milestones;
- estimate, budget baseline, reserves;
- Risk Register/responses;
- Quality Plan, Resource Plan, RACI;
- Communication/escalation/decision plan;
- Waterfall/Agile/Scrum/hybrid decision;
- monitoring dashboard dan EVM simulation;
- issue/change request simulation;
- Closure Report, transition, lessons;
- version history, traceability, sources, contribution.

## 4. Defense 12–15 Menit

```text
Problem/value → scope/deliverables → plan/baselines
→ risks/quality/governance → delivery approach
→ control/change simulation → closure/value → recommendation
```

Panel dapat memberi shock: budget -15%, vendor terlambat, regulation baru, key person keluar, security defect, atau deadline maju. Tim harus menganalisis dampak dan opsi, bukan menjawab “siap”.

## 5. 🏦 Verifikasi Industri

Untuk proyek fintech, panel menilai security/privacy/compliance, audit trail, vendor risk, incident/escalation, operational readiness, idempotency/reconciliation bila relevan, dan authority acceptance. Artefak tidak boleh membocorkan credential/data pribadi.

## 6. Rubrik UAS

| Komponen | Bobot |
|---|---:|
| Charter, stakeholder, value | 15% |
| Scope/WBS/schedule/budget | 25% |
| Risk/quality/resource/communication | 20% |
| Approach/governance | 10% |
| Monitoring/change/closure | 15% |
| Integrasi/defense/profesionalisme | 15% |

## 7. ⚠️ Risiko/Kesalahan Fatal

- Artefak saling bertentangan.
- Angka/tanggal tanpa basis.
- Template diisi tetapi tidak dipahami.
- Risiko kritis disembunyikan.
- Data/credential rahasia disertakan.
- Plagiarisme atau atribusi hilang.
- Tim tidak mampu menjelaskan keputusan.

## 8. Checklist Handover

- [ ] Final PMP dengan daftar isi/version.
- [ ] Spreadsheet/source artefak.
- [ ] Dashboard dan simulation.
- [ ] Evidence/assumption/source.
- [ ] Link bekerja dan akses tepat.
- [ ] Data sensitif disanitasi.
- [ ] Slide/PDF fallback.
- [ ] Kontribusi dan penggunaan AI dicatat.

## 9. 🧪 Pertanyaan Viva

Apa value dan success? Apa critical path? Estimate paling lemah? Top risk dan trigger? Siapa authority change? Mengapa pendekatan dipilih? Bagaimana mengukur progress? Apa kondisi rollback/stop? Siapa memiliki benefit setelah closure?

## 10. 📌 Ringkasan Akhir

- Project management menghubungkan strategy, people, delivery, control, dan value.
- Artefak harus traceable dan konsisten.
- Baseline bukan janji palsu; ia dasar transparansi/control.
- Risk/change perlu governance dan pilihan.
- Pendekatan harus di-tailor.
- Closure dan benefits sama pentingnya dengan delivery.
